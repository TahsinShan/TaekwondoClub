Our Legacy

More than martial arts. A way of life.

Established in 1998, Pappu Raz Shotokan Karate Academy has spent over 25 years of martial arts legacy building physical fitness, confidence, mental resilience, and strong character through structured training.

The academy was founded by Sensei M. S. Pappu Raz, Founder and Chief Instructor, and welcomes children, teenagers, adults, men and women.

DisciplineBuild focus, consistency, and self-control.
StrengthDevelop fitness, coordination, and resilience.
RespectPractice humility, responsibility, and mutual respect.
